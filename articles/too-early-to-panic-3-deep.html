Is it too early to panic for 3-Deep?

A top-three offense and a bottom-of-the-league defense have combined for the league's only winless record

By Larry Kuppleton

Fieldston Core Four Fantasy League · August 4, 2026

Zero and seven doesn't lie, even when the box score tries to.

San Francisco 3-Deep has scored 122.1 points a game this season — a mark that would sit comfortably in the league's top three. And yet here they sit, 0-7, the only team in the league that hasn't found a way to close one out.

The instinct is to call this bad luck. It isn't.

3-Deep is allowing 139.9 points a night, worst in the league by nearly 12 points over the next-closest team. Last in rebounding. Last in steals. Last in blocks. Not close to last — dead last, in all three.

The frustrating part, if you're San Francisco, is that the fix has been sitting on the bench the whole time. You don't lose seven straight because your stars underperform. You lose seven straight when your best two-way pieces are watching from the bench while the team gets outrebounded by 20.

So — too early to panic? Statistically, no team is technically eliminated at 0-7. There's time. But time isn't the same as a plan, and 3-Deep needs to prove it has one before this season quietly becomes a lost one.
