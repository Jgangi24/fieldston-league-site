League Rules

Welcome back boys, Season 2 is a whole new game!

The basics

  • 4 GMs, 2 teams each: 8 teams in all
  • Fresh snake draft. Every player drafted is assigned to one of your two teams
  • 12 players per team, plus up to 2 injured-reserve (IR) spots for players the commissioner approves
  • Every player on your roster counts, no matter how many minutes they play. There is no lineup or bench in the scoring
  • Injuries are on this year

The season

  • 14 weeks. Each week is one "one week" sim in BBGM: 7 days, usually 7 games per team
  • 98 games per team. Over the 14 weeks every team plays every other team twice, including your own other team
  • The last week (week 14) runs to the end of the regular season
  • BBGM's schedule has a few off days, so now and then a team plays 6 games in a week. Wins and Losses can split between the two teams that week because of it, and that is expected

How a week is scored

Each week your team faces one opponent and compares totals in 11 categories, added up across your whole roster:

  • Points
  • Rebounds
  • Assists
  • Steals
  • Blocks
  • Three-pointers made
  • Turnovers (fewer is better)
  • Field goal % (total makes / total attempts for the week)
  • Free throw % (total makes / total attempts for the week)
  • Wins (more is better)
  • Losses (fewer is better)

Every category is worth 1 point. If two teams tie in a category, each gets half a point. A week's result is your category record, like 7-3-1 (7 won, 3 lost, 1 tied).

Standings

Teams are ranked by total category points over the season (a tie counts half). If teams are tied, head-to-head matchups between them decide it (if three or more are tied, only their games against each other count), then total points scored.

Playoffs

  • The top 6 teams make it
  • Playoffs are a separate 6-week BBGM season in 3 rounds. Each round is 2 weeks of combined stats compared in the same 11 categories (FG% and FT% come from the combined shots). The team that wins more categories advances; a tie goes to the higher seed
  • Round 1: #3 vs #6 and #4 vs #5. #1 and #2 get a bye
  • Round 2: #1 plays the winner of #4/#5 and #2 plays the winner of #3/#6
  • Then the final

Add / drops

  • 1 add/drop per week per GM, submitted on the site (tap Add on a free agent, then choose who to drop)
  • Waiver order is the reverse of the standings: the lowest-ranked team picks first. The first waivers run after week 1 is played
  • If two GMs want the same player, the better waiver priority wins

Lineups, playing time and IR

  • You can reorder your starters and bench and set playing time on the site as often as you like. This doesn't change your score directly, but it changes the minutes your players get in BBGM, which changes their stats
  • Players the commissioner marks IR-eligible can be moved between active and IR whenever you want
  • Everything you change on the site is entered into BBGM by the commissioner before the next sim

The site

  • Matchups, standings and the full schedule are under the Season tab
  • Player and team stats, rankings and rivalries are under the Stats tab
  • Draft results and past seasons (More > History) live under More
  • The commissioner simulates a week at a time and updates the site

Anything that seems off, let's talk it through. These rules are subject to change.
