Game of the Week

Five-deep Blankendorf outlasts Oscar's masterpiece

Five players in double figures survive a 25-point, 12-assist night from Robertson in a game that came down to the final possessions

By Larry Kuppleton

Fieldston Core Four Fantasy League · August 4, 2026

In a league built on stars, sometimes the team with the deepest bench still wins the night.

Blankendorf got 21 from Carmelo Anthony, 19 from John Havlicek, 18 apiece from Bob Cousy and Vince Carter, and 17 more from Russell Westbrook — five players in double figures, no hero required — and it was enough to survive a genuine virtuoso performance from the other bench, Oscar Robertson's 25 points and 12 assists going for naught in a 122-119 Oakland loss.

“That's the thing about a team like ours,” Cousy said afterward, still catching his breath. “Nobody needs to have the best game of their life. We just need five guys to have a good one.”

Robertson had more than a good one. Playing all but a handful of minutes, he carved up the Blankendorf defense with the kind of complete performance that used to be reserved for whispered comparisons to the greats — and on this particular night, was simply the best anyone on either roster did. It still wasn't enough.

Scottie Pippen chipped in 18 of his own, adding seven rebounds and five assists in a complete two-way effort, and Hakeem Olajuwon found his footing inside for 18 points on 7-of-16 shooting. Tim Duncan, characteristically efficient, needed only six shots to get to 13 points, pulling down eight boards along the way.

But Kawhi Leonard's night told a different story. Twenty-two points arrived on 6-of-15 shooting and 3-of-10 from three, and five turnovers kept slipping through a game that Oakland could have used him controlling instead of chasing.

The bright spot off the bench was unmistakable. Jayson Tatum, playing just 17.7 minutes, went 4 of 6 from the field and 3 of 4 from three for 12 points, adding two blocks for good measure.

In the end, balance beat brilliance. Oakland will wonder what a cleaner night from Leonard might have meant. Blankendorf won't wonder anything at all.
